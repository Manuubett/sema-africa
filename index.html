Good to know

Frequently asked questions about learning Swahili

How long does it take to learn Swahili?

With one lesson a week plus short daily practice, most learners can handle greetings, shopping and simple conversations within two to three months. Reaching comfortable conversation usually takes six to twelve months.

Is Swahili hard for English speakers?

Swahili is one of the more approachable African languages. Pronunciation is regular and it uses the Latin alphabet. The main challenge is noun classes and verb prefixes, which we teach step by step.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No. Lessons start from your level, whether you are a complete beginner or already know some Swahili.

How do the live online lessons work?

You book a time that suits your time zone and meet your teacher live online. Every lesson is recorded so you can replay it, and you receive notes and exercises.

Can I learn Swahili for travel to Kenya or Tanzania?

Yes. We teach practical Swahili for markets, transport, restaurants, offices and family visits, so you can use it as soon as you arrive.

What does a lesson cost?

A 25-minute trial lesson is $5, a single 45-minute lesson is $12, and monthly plans are $40 for 4 lessons or $75 for 8 lessons. Prices are also shown in Kenyan shillings.
